Retro Meme-Stock Lists, 1926–2025

Companion data · “A Century of Meme Stocks and the Price of Coordination”

Monthly and weekly top-25 “meme stock” rankings back to 1926, from a classifier trained on the post-2020 meme era using market data alone (returns, options, short interest, retail flow — no social media, no text) and applied backward through history. Six model families, each restricted to the data available from its break year; browse any period, or look up a stock’s every appearance.

Trend Slope and Strength: Portfolio Returns, 1963–2025

Companion data · “Trend Slope and Strength” (with Kingsley Yeon)

Daily and monthly returns to the TS factor — long smooth uptrends, short noisy downtrends — in four specifications, all 25 cells of the conditional R2-then-slope double sort, rolling 60-month FF6 alphas, and decile spreads for fourteen benchmark signals built with the same machinery. Sort breakpoints and per-cell statistics are included so the sort can be reproduced with your own CRSP access; no stock-level data are redistributed.
