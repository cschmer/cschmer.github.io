A century of meme stocks, top 25 at a time

Monthly and weekly top-25 “meme stock” rankings, 1926–2025, from a classifier trained on the post-2020 meme era using market data alone — no social media, no text — then applied backward through history. Each model family uses only data available from its break year; Mmax stitches the richest family per era. See methodology, caveats, and CSV downloads.
