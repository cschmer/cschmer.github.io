Data · Trend Slope and Strength

Smooth uptrends, noisy downtrends, and the TS factor

Portfolio returns behind Trend Slope and Strength (Schmerling and Yeon). Each stock’s one-year price chart is regressed on time; the R2 is trend strength and the coefficient is trend slope. Stocks are sorted into R2 quintiles first and slope quintiles within, held for 21 trading days. The TS factor is long the smoothest uptrends (RQ5/SQ5) and short the noisiest downtrends (RQ1/SQ1). Four specifications: full CRSP universe or the largest 500 stocks, equal or value weighted, July 1963 to December 2025. See protocol, conventions, and CSV downloads.

Specification

The specification drives the heat map, rolling alpha, and summary table below. Full = CRSP common stocks priced at $5 or more; top-500 = largest 500 by market cap at formation; VW = value weight, EW = equal weight.

Index benchmarks are long-only: S&P 500 constituents (CRSP), and NASDAQ-listed common stocks priced at $5 or more; VW weights by prior-day market cap, EW rebalances to equal weights monthly. Signal spreads are decile-10-minus-decile-1 portfolios on the full universe, every signal formed on the paper’s chart window [t−252, t], from July 1967. Equal-weighted NASDAQ is dominated by small stocks and is not investable at scale. All series are gross of transaction costs.

The 5×5 double sort

Columns are trend-strength quintiles (RQ1 noisiest to RQ5 smoothest); rows are slope quintiles within each column (SQ5 steepest up on top, SQ1 steepest down at the bottom). Full-sample statistics for each cell; the TS spread is the top-right cell minus the bottom-left.

Cell alphas are intercepts on returns in excess of the one-month T-bill; the TS spread is self-financing, so its alpha is the intercept on the raw spread. FF6 = Fama–French five factors plus momentum, daily data, Newey–West standard errors with 21 lags.

Rolling 60-month FF6 alpha

At each month-end, the TS spread is regressed on the six factors over the trailing 60 months of daily returns. The upper panel is the annualized alpha; the lower panel is its t-statistic, with the ±1.96 band shaded.
