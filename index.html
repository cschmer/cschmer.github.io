Research

Slope, Strength, and Retail Extrapolation

Working Paper

Survey evidence suggests that investors use trend smoothness when forming return expectations, a feature momentum and other path-agnostic measures overlook. To test whether these extrapolative beliefs are priced, I regress cumulative returns on time, extracting trend slope and strength. A strategy that longs smooth uptrends and shorts noisy downtrends yields significant alpha beyond Fama-French plus momentum in value-weighted CRSP, 1962–2025. Predictable fundamentals generate the smoothness. Extrapolators then follow these paths, not news, so the premium concentrates among low-institutional-ownership stocks and the same stocks exhibit stronger post-earnings-announcement drift as news diffuses gradually into prices.
